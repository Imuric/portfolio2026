Famli App

Making complex family finances feel approachable by turning data into clear, intuitive experiences.

Team Product Design Lead, 2 Designers
Role Strategy, UX/UI, Research
Duration 4 Months

The Problem

Families struggled to get a single, intuitive view of investments, insurance, and wealth planning across multiple advisors and products. The existing solutions were fragmented, leading to decision paralysis and a lack of trust in their financial future.

User Insight

"I have money in five different places, and I have no idea if we're actually on track for retirement or just saving blindly."

The Solution

We built a modular dashboard that layered clarity over complexity, focusing on progressive disclosure and plain-language framings.

One-View Money

A unified dashboard that aggregates all family assets into a single, high-level overview, with the ability to drill down into specific categories.

  • Automatic bank & investment sync
  • Real-time asset allocation tracking
  • Shared family access for transparency

Goal-Based Planning

Moving away from complex spreadsheets to simple, goal-oriented buckets that help families save for what matters most.

  • Dynamic goal forecasting
  • Risk-adjusted recommendations
  • Automated monthly contributions

Key Outcomes

The new experience significantly improved user engagement and trust in the platform.

✅

28% Increase in Decision Velocity

Tested households were able to make financial decisions 28% faster thanks to the unified view.

✅

7.6 Trust Score

User trust scores on 'I understand my money' rose from 4.2 to 7.6 in post-launch surveys.

✅

Double Activation

Activation on goal-planning flows doubled within the first month of release.
