B2B Scale Marketplace

OTC Marketplace Seller Portal

Empowering sellers to list products and process orders at scale through a self-serve platform.

Client OTC Marketplace
Role Product Designer
Impact 20x Seller Growth

The Problem

Sellers had no self-serve path to list products, manage inventory, and process orders. Every single update had to be funneled through an internal operations team, creating massive bottlenecks as the marketplace tried to scale.

Ops Bottleneck

"Listing a new product takes 2 days because we have to wait for the support team to approve and upload our CSV."

Self-Serve Listings

A robust bulk-upload and manual entry system that gives sellers complete control over their catalog.

  • Real-time validation
  • Bulk image processing
  • Inventory sync

Impact & Outcomes

Metric Before After
Time-to-list 48 Hours 12 Minutes
Sellers Scaled 10 236
Support Tickets 100% Manual 64% Reduction
