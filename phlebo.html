Phlebotomist App

Optimizing field operations for home diagnostics through a thumb-first native mobile experience.

Client Thyrocare
Role Lead Designer
Platform Android Native

The Problem

Phlebotomists were struggling with a legacy app that was hard to use with one hand while managing medical kits. This led to long visit times, data entry errors, and high cancellation rates due to customer frustration.

Field Reality

"I have to use my phone while standing in a busy hallway. The buttons are too small and the app crashes when I lose signal."

Thumb-First Interface

Redesigned all core workflows to be accessible within the 'thumb zone' for easy one-handed use in the field.

  • Large touch targets
  • Bottom-aligned actions
  • High-contrast visuals

Impact & Outcomes

⏱️

45 → 25 min Visit Time

Drastically reduced the average turnaround time per home visit.

📉

50% Lower Cancellations

Better state cues and customer script prompts improved the overall experience.
